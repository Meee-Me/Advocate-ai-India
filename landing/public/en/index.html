Safeguards

AI can be wrong, so these checks are built into the app.

Guards against invented authorities

Reporter citations not found in material you supplied are removed by default; case names and Acts are marked unverified.

Quotes are checked

A quote in an analysis that does not appear word for word in the document is shown as "not found".

Each advocate’s data is separate

Every record is scoped to the advocate’s account; PDFs are kept in private storage.

Clear about what goes to the AI

Case text and documents you analyse are sent to the AI provider (Ollama Cloud). OCR itself runs on our own server.

Before you add client material, confirm this fits your confidentiality duties and the DPDP Act, 2023.
